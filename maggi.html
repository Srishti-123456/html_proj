🍜Hakka noodles

  • Prep time:20min
  • Cook time:15min
  • Servings:4
  • Difficulty:Easy

Ingredients

  • 250g Hakka noodles
  • 2tbps Vegetable oil
  • 1tbps Black pepper powder
  • 2tbps Soya sauce
  • 1tbps Vinegar
  • 1tbps Red or Green chilli sauce

    • HOW TO MAKE

      steps
    1. Boil it in Frying pan and put salt,oil to add taste
    2. Now strain it and keep for sometime
    3. now cut Vegetables in fine pieces
      Vegetables
    1. 🥕Carrot
    2. 🌶Green chilli
    3. 🥫Spring onion
    4. 🫑 Capsicum
    5. 🧅Onion
    6. 🧄Garlic
    7. 🫑 Ginger
    8. 🥬Cabage

    Now How to cook

    • First take oil in pan and then put ginger,garlic and onion.now cook until it turns into golden brown
    • Add noodles,and add saltand Black pepper powder.Also add 1tbps vinegar,1tbps Red or Green chilli sauce with also a 2tbps Soya sauce and now mix it well
      • Now add vegetables and mix well
      • After sometime it will be fully cooked ,So now it isready to serve
